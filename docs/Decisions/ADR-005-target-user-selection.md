\# ADR-005: Target User Selection



\## Status



Accepted



\---



\## Context



The product can potentially serve many user groups including students, researchers, teachers, and professionals.



Supporting all users from the beginning would significantly increase product complexity and dilute the MVP.



\---



\## Decision



The MVP will focus primarily on university students.



Future versions may expand to support:



\* Competitive exam aspirants

\* Researchers

\* Teachers

\* Working professionals



\---



\## Rationale



University students:



\* experience the problem frequently,

\* have large amounts of study material,

\* benefit greatly from personalization,

\* are easier to interview and validate.



\---



\## Consequences



\### Positive



\* Clear product focus.

\* Easier feature prioritization.

\* Better MVP definition.

\* Faster feedback cycle.



\### Negative



\* Some potential users are excluded initially.

\* Certain features may need redesign during future expansion.
