# ADR-010: Product Success Definition

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

The product aims to solve educational inefficiencies rather than simply provide another AI chatbot.

---

## Decision

The primary measure of success will be:

Improving learning efficiency.

---

## Product Principle

Help students learn the right things at the right depth in the least amount of time.

---

## Consequences

### Positive

* Clear product focus.
* Strong feature prioritization.
* Better differentiation.

### Negative

* Measuring educational outcomes may require user feedback and surveys.
