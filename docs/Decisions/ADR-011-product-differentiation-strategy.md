# ADR-011: Product Differentiation Strategy

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

The market already contains:

* General AI assistants
* AI document assistants
* Knowledge management tools

Competing solely on document chat is insufficient.

---

## Decision

The platform will differentiate through:

1. Syllabus-aware learning.
2. Assessment-aware explanations.
3. Personalized learning context.
4. Workspace-based organization.
5. Affordable AI options.

---

## Product Principle

Compete on educational context rather than generic AI capabilities.

---

## Consequences

### Positive

* Strong differentiation.
* Clear product positioning.
* Better alignment with user needs.

### Negative

* Increased product complexity.
* Requires additional metadata and processing pipelines.
