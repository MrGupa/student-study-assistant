# ADR-009: Problem Prioritization

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

User research revealed that students do not primarily struggle because study material is unavailable.

The primary challenge is efficiently learning from an overwhelming amount of information within limited time constraints.

---

## Decision

The product will prioritize:

1. Learning efficiency.
2. Information organization.
3. Syllabus awareness.
4. Topic prioritization.

The product will not initially focus on becoming a generic tutoring platform.

---

## Consequences

### Positive

* Strong product differentiation.
* Clear MVP scope.
* Better feature prioritization.

### Negative

* Some advanced tutoring capabilities become lower priority.

---

## Product Principle

Help students learn the right things at the right depth within their limited time. Prioritize learning efficiency over content volume.
