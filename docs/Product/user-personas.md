# User Personas

# Purpose

This document defines the primary and secondary user personas for the Student Study Assistant platform. These personas guide product decisions, feature prioritization, UX design, and future roadmap planning.

---

# Persona 1: University Student (Primary Persona)

## Name

Rahul Sharma (Fictional)

## Age

18-24

## Role

Undergraduate Engineering Student

## Technical Comfort

Medium to High

## Study Materials

* PDFs
* PPTs
* Lecture Notes
* Previous Year Papers
* YouTube Videos
* Blogs and Articles
* Web Resources

## Study Pattern

* Studies subject-by-subject.
* Uses the platform daily or weekly.
* Uses the platform heavily during examinations.
* Frequently works on assignments and revision.
* Frequently studies under time constraints and prioritizes high-weightage topics.

## Goals

* Learn concepts faster.
* Understand difficult topics.
* Reduce study time.
* Keep all study materials organized.
* Stay within syllabus boundaries.
* Revise efficiently.

## Biggest Pain Points

1. Study materials are scattered.
2. Information overload.
3. Difficulty finding relevant information quickly.
4. Uncertainty about how deeply a topic needs to be studied.
5. Difficulty identifying important topics.
6. Constant context switching between tools.

## Constraints

* Limited budget.
* Limited time.
* Multiple subjects.
* Large amounts of information.

## Desired AI Capabilities

* Search personal materials.
* Explain concepts.
* Summarize topics.
* Syllabus-aware learning.
* Assignment assistance.
* Topic importance indicators.
* Deeper explanations on demand.

## Success Criteria

* Reduced study time.
* Better understanding.
* Faster revision.
* Better exam performance.

## Representative Quote

"I know the material exists somewhere, but I don't know where it is, what is important, or how deeply I need to study it."

# Persona 2: Competitive Exam Aspirant

## Name

Aman Verma (Fictional)

## Age

20-24

## Role

Final-Year Engineering Student preparing for GATE.

## Technical Comfort

Medium to High

## Study Duration

* 1-2 years of preparation.
* Large and deep syllabus.

## Study Materials

* Coaching Notes
* Standard Textbooks
* Previous Papers
* Question Banks
* PDFs
* YouTube Videos
* Online Articles

## Study Pattern

* Long-term preparation.
* Frequent revision.
* Repeated testing and practice.
* Progress tracking.

## Goals

* Complete the syllabus on time.
* Retain knowledge.
* Improve problem-solving ability.
* Identify weak topics.
* Maximize exam score.

## Biggest Pain Points

1. Massive syllabus.
2. Information overload.
3. Difficulty tracking progress.
4. Forgetting previously studied topics.
5. Knowing what to revise next.
6. Applying knowledge to problems.

## Constraints

* Limited preparation time.
* Long preparation periods.
* Large number of subjects.
* Need for continuous revision.

## Desired AI Capabilities

* Study plans.
* Revision reminders.
* Spaced repetition.
* Weak topic detection.
* Progress analytics.
* Personalized quizzes.
* Adaptive testing.

## Success Criteria

* Better retention.
* Higher mock test scores.
* Consistent progress.
* Improved confidence before examinations.

## Representative Quote

"I know what I need to study, but I don't know what I should revise next or whether I'm actually improving."

# Persona 3: Research Student

## Name

Priya Iyer (Fictional)

## Age

22-30

## Role

Master's Student / Research Assistant

## Technical Comfort

High

## Study Materials

* Research Papers
* Journal Articles
* Conference Papers
* Books
* Technical Reports
* Datasets

## Study Pattern

* Reads large collections of documents.
* Compares papers.
* Creates notes and summaries.
* Produces research reports and papers.

## Goals

* Understand literature quickly.
* Compare approaches.
* Organize references.
* Generate accurate summaries.
* Produce well-documented research.

## Biggest Pain Points

1. Too many papers to read.
2. Information overload.
3. Difficulty comparing papers.
4. Remembering sources.
5. Citation management.
6. Synthesizing information from multiple sources.

## Constraints

* Need for accuracy.
* Need for source verification.
* High documentation requirements.
* Large amount of information.

## Desired AI Capabilities

* Paper summarization.
* Cross-document reasoning.
* Citation support.
* Source attribution.
* Literature review generation.
* Research note management.
* Paper comparison.

## Success Criteria

* Faster literature reviews.
* Fewer citation mistakes.
* Better organization.
* Improved research productivity.

## Representative Quote

"I know the information exists somewhere in my papers, but I can't remember where I read it or how it connects to other research."

# Persona 4: Teacher / Educator

Dr. Meera Patel (Fictional)

## Age

30-55

## Role

University Professor / Lecturer

## Technical Comfort

Medium

## Teaching Materials

* Lecture Notes
* PPTs
* Question Banks
* Previous Papers
* Books
* Research Papers

→ Deliver Lecture
→ Assess Students
→ Improve Material

## Goals

* Generate assessments quickly.
* Keep materials organized.
* Ensure syllabus coverage.
* Share learning resources.

2. Preparing assignments repeatedly.
3. Organizing teaching materials.
4. Reusing old content.
5. Ensuring syllabus coverage.

## Constraints

* Limited preparation time.
* Large amount of content.
* Administrative work.

## Desired AI Capabilities

* Quiz generation.
* Assignment generation.
* Question bank generation.
* Lecture summarization.
* Material organization.
* Syllabus tracking.
* Resource sharing.

## Success Criteria

* Reduced preparation time.
* Better organization.
* Faster assessment creation.

## Representative Quote

"I spend too much time preparing teaching material that I have already created in some form before."
