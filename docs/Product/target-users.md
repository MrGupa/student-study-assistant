\# Target Users



\## Purpose



This document defines the intended users of the Student Study Assistant platform and helps guide product decisions, feature prioritization, and future roadmap planning.



\---



\# User Segmentation Strategy



The platform follows a phased user strategy:



1\. Primary Users (MVP)

2\. Secondary Users (Future Expansion)

3\. Tertiary Users (Long-Term Vision)

4\. Non-Target Users (Out of Scope)



\---



\# Primary Users (MVP)



\## University Students



\### Description



Undergraduate and postgraduate students who manage large amounts of academic material and need an efficient way to organize, understand, and learn from it.



\### Characteristics



\* Study multiple subjects simultaneously.

\* Have notes distributed across many formats.

\* Frequently revise before exams.

\* Need quick access to information.

\* Use AI tools to improve productivity.



\### Examples



\* Computer Engineering Students

\* Mechanical Engineering Students

\* Medical Students

\* Law Students

\* Business Students



\---



\## Why They Are The Primary Users



\### Problem Severity



High



\### Frequency of Use



Very High



\### Availability of Study Materials



High



\### Need for Personalization



High



\### Ease of User Research



High



\---



\## Primary Use Cases



\* Upload semester materials.

\* Ask questions from notes and books.

\* Search concepts quickly.

\* Summarize topics.

\* Prepare for exams.

\* Generate study aids.



\---



\# Secondary Users (Future Phases)



\## Competitive Exam Aspirants



\### Description



Students preparing for exams such as:



\* GATE

\* UPSC

\* GRE

\* CAT

\* JEE

\* NEET



\### Characteristics



\* Study from many resources.

\* Follow long-term study plans.

\* Need revision assistance.

\* Need progress tracking.



\### Potential Use Cases



\* Organizing preparation materials.

\* Topic-wise revision.

\* Personalized quizzes.

\* Weak topic identification.

\* Study planning.



\---



\## Researchers



\### Description



Students or academics working with large collections of research papers and reference material.



\### Characteristics



\* Read many documents.

\* Need citation support.

\* Need semantic search.

\* Need summarization.



\### Potential Use Cases



\* Research paper management.

\* Cross-document querying.

\* Literature reviews.

\* Knowledge organization.



\---



\# Tertiary Users (Long-Term Vision)



\## Teachers and Educators



\### Potential Use Cases



\* Organizing teaching materials.

\* Creating quizzes.

\* Creating study guides.

\* Preparing lecture summaries.



\---



\## Working Professionals



\### Potential Use Cases



\* Upskilling.

\* Learning new technologies.

\* Managing certification materials.

\* Creating personal knowledge bases.



\---



\# Non-Target Users (Current Scope)



The following users are intentionally out of scope:



\* Enterprise knowledge management teams

\* Corporate document management systems

\* Legal document review systems

\* Healthcare document management systems

\* General-purpose productivity users



These domains have significantly different requirements and are not aligned with the product's educational focus.

\# Ideal Early Adopters



The first users of the platform should be:



\* Engineering students

\* Computer Science students

\* Students with large amounts of digital study materials

\* Students already using AI tools for studying

\* Students preparing for semester examinations



These users experience the problem most frequently and can provide rapid feedback.

\* Revision assistance



\---



\## Competitive Exam Aspirants



Need:

\* Personalized revision

\* Progress tracking



\---



\## Researchers



Need:



\* Cross-document knowledge discovery

\* Summarization

\* Citation support



\---



\# Product Focus Statement



The Student Study Assistant platform is primarily designed for university students who struggle with fragmented study materials and need an intelligent, personalized learning workspace.



Future versions of the platform may expand to support additional educational and research-focused users.
