# Differentiators

## D0. Learning Efficiency

The platform is designed to help students:
learn the right things,
at the right depth,
within limited time.

## D1. Syllabus-Aware Learning

Tailors explanations according to syllabus boundaries.

---

## D2. Assessment-Aware Learning

Uses previous papers and marks distribution to prioritize topics.

---

## D3. Persistent Learning Context

Remembers:

* documents,
* preferences,
* learning style,
* workspace settings.

---

## D4. Workspace-Based Organization

Subject-specific learning environments.

---

## D5. Personalized Learning

Supports workspace-level preferences.

---

## D6. Affordable AI Options

* Local LLM support.
* Bring-your-own API keys.
* Hosted service.

---

# Positioning Statement

Student Study Assistant is a personalized, syllabus-aware learning workspace that helps students organize their study materials and learn the right things at the right depth within limited time.

---

# SWOT Analysis

## Strengths

* Syllabus awareness
* Personalized learning
* Topic prioritization
* Workspace organization
* Cost flexibility

## Weaknesses

* Higher engineering complexity
* Dependence on document quality
* Requires onboarding

## Opportunities

* University students
* Competitive exam aspirants
* Researchers
* Teachers

## Threats

* General AI assistants
* AI note-taking platforms
* Educational platforms adding AI features
