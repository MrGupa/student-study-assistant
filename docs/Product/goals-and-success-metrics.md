# Goals and Success Metrics

# Purpose

This document defines the goals, success criteria, and measurable outcomes of the Student Study Assistant platform.

---

# Product Vision

To become a personalized AI learning workspace that helps students learn more effectively and efficiently.

---

# North Star

Help students learn the right things at the right depth in the least amount of time.

---

# Product Mission

Reduce the time and effort required to:

* organize study materials,
* find information,
* understand concepts,
* revise efficiently,
* prepare for examinations.

---
