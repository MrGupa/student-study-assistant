\# Problem Statement



\## Background



Students often manage study materials across multiple sources and formats, including PDFs, PPTs, notes, books, assignments, and online resources.



This fragmentation makes studying inefficient and increases the time spent searching for information.



\---



\## Primary Problem



Students struggle to study efficiently because they have an overwhelming amount of information, limited preparation time, and insufficient context about what to study and how deeply to study it.



\---



\## Secondary Problems



\* Difficulty finding information quickly.

\* Lack of personalized learning assistance.

\* Inefficient revision and exam preparation.

\* Difficulty remembering previously learned concepts.

\* Time-consuming note creation and summarization.

\* Lack of a centralized learning workspace.



\---



\## Existing Solutions and Their Limitations



Generic AI tools can answer questions but:



\* do not understand the student's complete knowledge base,

\* do not retain learning progress,

\* do not personalize responses,

\* do not provide long-term learning support,

\* can become expensive for students.



\---



\## Proposed Solution



Provide an AI-powered learning workspace that allows students to:



\* organize study materials,

\* search information instantly,

\* chat with their knowledge base,

\* generate study aids,

\* receive personalized learning assistance.



\---



\## Success Criteria



The system should reduce the time students spend:



\* searching for information,

\* organizing materials,

\* preparing for examinations,

\* creating study aids.
