\# Product Vision



\## Product Name



Student Study Assistant (Working Title)



\---



\## Vision Statement



To become a personalized AI learning companion that helps students organize, understand, and master knowledge from their own study materials throughout their learning journey.



\---



\## Mission Statement



To provide students with an intelligent learning workspace that centralizes academic materials and uses AI to improve understanding, retention, and productivity.



\---



\## One-Sentence Product Description



An AI-powered knowledge workspace that helps students learn from their own study materials through search, conversation, and personalized learning assistance.



\---



\## Product Category



\* AI-Powered Learning Workspace

\* Personal Knowledge Management System

\* Personalized AI Study Assistant



\---



\## Long-Term Vision



Build a complete AI learning operating system that supports students from content organization and learning to assessment, revision, and continuous improvement.



\---



\## Core Principles



1\. Learning should be personalized.

2\. Study materials should be centralized.

3\. AI should assist understanding, not replace learning.

4\. Students should have affordable AI options.

5\. The system should adapt to each student's learning needs.

6\. Learning should be efficient and context-aware.
